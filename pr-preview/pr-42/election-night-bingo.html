Election night is long. The polls close, the maps light up, and then everyone watches an anchor tap a touchscreen for four hours. A bingo card gives the room something to do together besides refresh their phones, and it works whoever you're rooting for, because none of the squares care who wins.

Why Every Square Is About the Broadcast

"Too close to call." The giant map. A victory speech that runs long. These happen on every channel, every election, so a card full of them fills up at the same pace no matter what the results are. There are no candidate names, no party colours and no predictions anywhere on it. Bring it to a room with mixed politics and it still works, which is the whole point of a party game.

Share Your Card

The Share button turns your card, marks and all, into a story-sized picture with the link on it. On a phone it opens your share sheet, so it goes straight to your Instagram or TikTok story, Facebook, or a group chat. Your friends tap the link and get their own card. Hit BINGO and the picture says so.

Running a blog or a newsroom live page? Open "Put this playable card on your own site" under the card for an embed code. Each of your readers gets their own card too.
