New Year's Eve Bingo: Free Printable Party Cards

The hardest part of a New Year's Eve party is the long middle: dinner's done, midnight is hours away, and the conversation needs a nudge. A bingo card gives the evening a shape. Here are three lists: one for the party, one for kids who won't make it to midnight, and one to carry into January.

How to Run It

Calling the kids' list

Paste the 30 words into a document, print the page, and snip it into slips to draw from a party hat. Read each one aloud and keep the drawn slips in order so you can check a winning card.

Prizes

Noisemakers, a glow stick, first pick of the snack table, or the job of leading the countdown.

Printing and phones

Landscape fits two cards on a page with a cut line. To skip printing, use Share Online: every guest opens their own card on their phone, and a pen and a napkin handle the marking. Designing and printing cost nothing; free downloads include a small demo watermark, which a pass removes.
