Office Holiday Party Bingo: Free Cards for Teams

Every work holiday party has the same problem: people stand with the colleagues they already sit beside. Bingo is a gentle fix. It gives everyone a reason to cross the room, takes no setup, and works whether the party is in a conference room or on a video call. Here are three ready-made lists.

📅 October 2026 ⏱ 7 min read ✍️ BingoCardGenerator.Online

Which List Fits Your Party?

  • Find a coworker who...: a mixer. Players find a different colleague for each square and write their name in it. Best in the first 20 minutes, while people are still arriving.
  • Party moments: runs on its own once the mixer ends; players mark what they see.
  • Remote party: the same idea for a video call, built from the moments every online meeting shares.

How to Run It at Work

Timing

The find-a-coworker card works best early, while people are arriving and the room is still quiet. Give it a firm 15 minutes. The moments card can then run until the end without anyone needing to host it.

Prizes

Keep them light: a coffee gift card, first pick in the gift exchange, an extra half day if your manager is feeling generous, or the honor of choosing next year's party theme.

Printing

Landscape printing puts two cards on each page with a cut line, so a stack for 40 people is only 20 sheets. Bring pens; nobody has one at a party.

Cards are free to design and print, with a small demo watermark on free downloads. A pass removes the watermark and lets you add your company logo to every card.

Want your office's in-jokes on the card? Type them into any list first, and every card picks them up.
