How to Run It

Calling the Classic and Little Elves lists

Paste the list into a document, print it, and cut the words apart. Put the slips in a stocking or a hat. Draw one at a time, read it out, and set it aside. When someone calls bingo, check their marked squares against the drawn pile.

Patterns for a longer game

  • One line for a fast first round.
  • Four corners to replay with the cards you already have.
  • Blackout for the last round of the night.

Small prizes

A candy cane, first pick from the cookie tin, an extra marshmallow in the cocoa, or the right to choose tonight's movie.

Printing and Phones

Choose portrait for one big card per page, which is easier for small hands, or landscape for two cards per page with a cut line. If a few relatives are joining by video, use Share Online and send each of them a link to their own card, which opens on a phone or tablet.
