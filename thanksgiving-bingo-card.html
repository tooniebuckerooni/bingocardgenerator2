Thanksgiving dinner runs on the same moments every year. Somebody is running late, grace goes long, the pie debate starts before the plates are cleared. A bingo card turns all of it into a game the whole table can play, from the kids' table to the grandparents dialling in on video.

A Card for Every Seat

Send the link to the family group chat before dinner. Everyone who opens it gets their own card, dealt from a pool of 30 squares, so people fill different lines at different times and nobody wins on the same square. No account, no app, nothing to print.

Share Your Card

The Share button turns your card, marks and all, into a story-sized picture with the link on it. On a phone it opens your share sheet, so it goes straight to your Instagram or TikTok story, Facebook, or a group chat. Hit BINGO and the picture says so.
