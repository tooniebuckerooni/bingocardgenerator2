How to Make Custom Bingo Cards

A custom bingo card is just a regular card built from your own content instead of a pre-made template — your words, your theme, your look. That's the whole idea: instead of adapting your event to a generic set of squares, you build the card around what you're actually doing.

1
Write your own squaresType at least 25 words, phrases, or trivia questions — anything specific to your classroom, party, or event. There's no fixed list to work around.
2
Personalize the designPick a font, a color theme, and a card title. A Pro pass also unlocks a logo or custom branding text on every card, for a fully on-brand set.
3
Choose free space and layoutToggle the center free space on or off, then pick portrait for one card per page or landscape for two with a cut line.
4
Download or shareDownload a print-ready PDF, a ZIP of card images, or generate individual shareable links players open on their own phones.

Minimum 25 squares. A 5×5 card needs 25 items — words, phrases, trivia questions, anything. Import more than 25 if you want less repetition across a full set of cards.

What Makes a Bingo Card "Custom"

Your own words or theme

Vocabulary words, inside jokes, trivia questions, or song titles — the squares are whatever your event needs, not a fixed list.

Pick fonts & colors
